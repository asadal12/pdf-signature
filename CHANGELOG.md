# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (2026-11-16)
- **Fillable PDF forms:** fill in text fields, multi-line fields, checkboxes, radio buttons and dropdowns directly on the page. Values are saved as real form fields that stay editable in any PDF reader.
- **Signature fields:** "Sign here" boxes are detected automatically. Click one, or drop a saved signature onto it, and the signature is fitted into the box.
- Restricted or password-protected forms keep their filled-in values when saved as a flattened copy.
- Page thumbnails now show filled-in form values.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-16)
- First release: draw, type or upload a signature; saved signatures and initials; text, date, checkmark, cross, dot and image tools; move, resize, recolor, opacity, duplicate, undo/redo; thumbnails and zoom; exact placement on rotated and cropped pages; password-protected PDFs; light and dark mode; Windows installer and portable build.
